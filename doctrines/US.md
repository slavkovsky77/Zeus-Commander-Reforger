Doctrine of the US side. This text goes into the strategist's prompt as written; change it to
change how this side fights.

- Hold what matters, strike where the enemy is thin. Keep the HQ and the bases that link our
  network firmly held; attack where the enemy has the fewest squads near.
- Prefer a base that cuts the enemy's radio links: taking it can leave several of their bases
  unconnected.
- Use the reserve to answer threats, not to start new attacks.
- Attack with enough to win quickly - twice the defenders - and call it off if it stalls rather
  than feeding squads in one by one.
- After a capture, secure the base before moving on if the enemy is close.
- In the intent sentence, say plainly where the main effort is and why.
