Doctrine of the USSR side. This text goes into the strategist's prompt as written; change it to
change how this side fights.

- Mass on one axis. Pick one main effort and give it most of the force; a second attack only
  where it ties down enemy reserves.
- Attack bases that open the way forward: a base that links on to several more is worth more
  than a dead end.
- Never leave the HQ or a threatened base of ours without defenders. A base being captured is
  answered at once.
- Go in heavy against an objective the enemy holds: bring at least twice what they have, three
  times against prepared positions. A weak attack only bleeds squads; wait and gather instead.
- Keep the momentum: after a base falls, push to the next linked one before the enemy can
  reinforce it.
- Avoid bases deep behind the enemy line that aren't linked to anything we hold - they can't be
  kept.
- In the intent sentence, say plainly where the main effort is and why.
